Note that breadboard.png and perfboard.jpg do not show the final design, which is faithfully recorded in the
schematic.png.

In the final design, I also opted to go without the heatsink shown on the STP80NF70 mosfet in breadboard.png.

The photoresistor module shown in breadboard.png and perfboard.jpg was swapped out for a standalone through-hole
photoresistor with a 1K resistor forming a voltage divider to the ESP32 sense pin (pin 32).
